AsiaVerify Colour Tokens

The shared colour system for AsiaVerify products, in the format of the AsiaVerify colour sheets. Semantic tokens say what a colour is for; the raw palettes name the colours they point at; the Figma tab lists the variables with both modes. In code, content.main is --color-content-main; in Figma it is content/main. Click a swatch to copy it.

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.2 and computed from the values, so they can't drift from the hex. Text needs 4.5:1; UI edges and meaningful fills need 3:1. Translucent colours are shown over the surface they sit on. Names of new raw colours are proposals in the sheet's naming style. Source of truth: src/index.css, until the tokens move to a shared package. Regenerate with python3 docs/tokens/build.py.
